## What is Maintenance-Only mode?

It means that new features aren't prioritized, or simply won't be added to the app. But the reported bugs are likely to get fixed.

## Why entering Maintenance-Only mode?

I'm shifting my resources to the other projects.


The whole reason is the AI usage in the development of this program. I'm not repeating obvious things about AI here, as you might already know them.


The AI-assisted code affects the critical features of this application (RTMP, video overlay, video crop utility, hardware rotation detector and some custom widgets). They could be rewritten, but I'd rather not, as many people think that the AI code is permanent.


The real pity is that I didn't disclose the AI usage properly in time. The first AI-tainted commit to this repository was made in April 2026, but the disclosure message followed only in August.


So, may my AI _"experience"_ be a lesson to the other developers out there.
